# Link2Video Local Downloader Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy covers the Link2Video Microsoft Edge extension and its separately installed Windows helper. For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the support contact on the extension's Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing.

## Information used

When you click the extension button, Link2Video reads the active tab's URL to prefill the download form. You can also enter a video URL yourself. It uses your selected URL, video or audio option, and preferred quality to perform the download. It does not continuously monitor browsing or read the browser's history database, passwords, browser cookies, or messages.

The extension sends the selected URL and download settings to the helper on your computer through native messaging. The helper retrieves video metadata and media from the selected platform and its content delivery services. These requests expose your IP address, requested resource, and normal network request information to those services, whose own privacy policies apply. Link2Video does not send download requests or media through a publisher-operated server.

## Local storage and display

Downloaded media and associated file metadata are stored on your computer. Temporary working files are used during downloads. The helper stores local configuration, including the permitted extension ID, download location, and dependency paths. The extension displays the download location, progress, results, and error details. The selected URL is also included in the local extension page's URL to prefill the form.

## Developer collection and sharing

Link2Video contains no developer analytics, advertising, or tracking service. The publisher does not receive your selected links, browsing history, or downloaded media through the software. User data is not sold, used for creditworthiness or lending decisions, or used for purposes unrelated to the requested download. Transfers to the selected video platform and its delivery services are necessary to retrieve the requested content.

## Installation and updates

Helper setup and dependency updates can contact software distribution services, including the Python package index and Windows package sources, to download required components. Those services receive normal network request information and apply their own policies.

## Your choices

You control which link is downloaded and can cancel an active download. Delete downloaded files through Windows File Explorer. Uninstalling the extension removes the browser component; uninstall the helper separately using its uninstall command. Downloaded files remain until you delete them. Do not share download links containing information you do not intend the source platform to receive.

## Support and policy changes

If you contact the publisher for support, the publisher receives the information you choose to include. Remove private links and personal information from screenshots or logs before sending them. This policy will be updated when Link2Video's data practices change; the revision date will appear above.
